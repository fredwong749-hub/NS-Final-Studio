### 2.3 Arbitration mechanisms (all live at once)
1. **partition.json** gives each host its episodes for images and video, plus per-host lane lists, `retired_lanes`, `_temp_holds` and `_lent_lanes`. tools/netfix/dispatch.py turns it into each producer's `control.json` (`only` and `hold_lanes` = "other machine's lanes"). The push runs through resident_sync `push_control` → `server-control`.
2. **control.json hold_lanes** is a snapshot. producer `effective_holds` (producer.py:1371) re-derives it against partition, because stale snapshots used to block lanes (W44 ④).
3. **lane_roles.json** (L-206/207) defines per-phase image/review/video lanes. It is read by four modules:
   - producer `image_lanes_allowed` / `lanes_minus_resident_review` (producer.py:3511-3544)
   - night `roles_for_host` (night.py:86)
   - asset_worker `lane_roles_policy` (asset_worker.py:317)
   - review_instances.sh
4. **lane_roles_state.json `_active`** is the run-level "which episode owns the cards". It is written by **each host's producer independently** (producer.py:3462-3489 → lane_roles_producer.write_state).
5. **yield / shared_lanes / per_lane** (night DEFAULT_CTRL night.py:59, vidgen policy) means video hands lanes back to images after `yield_s`. Separately, producer `probe_lanes` puts its job behind at most one "yielding" foreign task (producer.py:3567-3572).
6. **video_boost vram_gate**, **video_lanes** (dedicated, no yield) and the **ep01_lane** special case are in night.py:1002-1006 and :344.
7. **video_shard.json** (ep01: owner gpu36, weights 3:2, exclude gpu33:3) + shard_claims.json, with shard_relay on the laptop as the go-between.
8. **lane_relay.json** + `lane_relay_hold.json` let the gpu33 producer use gpu36 lanes. The relay's own hold means "give back to gpu36 video".
9. **Leases** (producer data/producer/runs/<run>/leases) are per process. They are not shared across hosts. `work_stealing:false` (partition.json).
10. **Train locks** (`train_locks_cached`, producer.py:1405) and render_endpoints `hold`.
11. **Pauses**: control.paused, pause_shots (producer.py:4900), canary pause (:1293-1342), runaway/lookA/orphan/video hold files (C:L385, still on disk at 100+ h), and sop_gate backoff (per gap signature).
12. **asset_worker holds** write partition `_temp_holds` with a 15 min TTL and renewal (asset_worker.py:380). They are honoured by producer (`partition_held`) and by vidgen only through control.

### 2.4 Conflicts observed / structural
- **`_active` diverges by host.** At 23:30:
  - gpu33 lane_roles_state: ep1 phase=videos, ep2 frames switches=0, last ep2 change 18:53.
  - gpu36: ep1 phase=**frames**, ep2 switches=2 (prev videos).
  - Both claim `_active.ep=2`, but their per-episode phases disagree. L-207 "run-level single active episode" is computed twice, with no shared truth (rssh read of data/pipeline/lane_roles_state.json on both hosts; C:L509 "gpu33's lane_roles_state stopped at 18:53").
- **night's lane_roles never applied before the W-SCHED fix.** `roles_for_host` returned None while ep01 (< from_episode) was "ready" (night.py:104-112 comment; C:L509). L-206/207 were deployed but not in effect for the video side.
- **The review card gets image jobs.** For ep01 (not governed by lane_roles) `image_lanes_allowed` returned None, so "any free lane" included gpu33:3, and the VQA instance hit CUDA OOM (producer.py:3520-3523 comment; C:L492).
- **Lane relay and partition overlap.** partition says the gpu36 lanes belong to the gpu36 producer. The relay hands the same physical GPUs to the gpu33 producer. The two producers arbitrate only through ComfyUI queue inspection (`foreign` agents). Neither sees the other's leases (producer.py:3550-3556).
- **asset_worker (laptop) competes for image lanes.** It holds them through partition `_temp_holds` (see the live `_temp_holds` gpu33:0/gpu33:1 entries in data/state/netfix/partition.json). On 11-05 it held gpu33:0/1/3 during CPU-only scoring (C:L346).
- **Video sharding is a third split.** partition video gives ep→host. video_shard gives a per-shot owner/helper split. lane_roles gives the per-phase lanes. night merges all three in `_shard_hold` (night.py:404) and `apply_lane_policy`.
- **Hard-coded lane lists disagree with the tables.**
  - night `IMAGE_LANES` (night.py:937) omits gpu36:2.
  - DEFAULT_CTRL shared_lanes (night.py:59) includes it.
  - Before REVIEW-A, shared_lanes lacked gpu36:2 (C:L337).
  - The WITHDRAW port map is hard-coded (night.py:1014).
  - producer IMAGE_LANES is hard-coded (producer.py:135).
  - Before C:L343, asset_worker hard-coded the gpu36:2 exclusion.
- **Restart semantics are split.** A night-only restart did not restart vidgen (C:L333) and also stopped the producer (C:L460).

## 6. Consolidation candidates (short list, no design)

1. **One scheduler per GPU.** Today there are 7 submitters (2 producers, 2 vidgens, asset_worker, review scaling, research) and 12 arbitration mechanisms (§2.3). The built-but-off tools/sched/core.py is the natural single board.
2. **One owner host for run-level state.** Candidates: `_active` phase, partition, registry, takes. This removes per-host lane_roles_state, the two partition copies and the three registries.
3. **One lane table.** hosts.json should be the only source. Remove the hard-coded lists in producer.py:135, night.py:59/937/1014, render_endpoints `lanes`, and partition `lanes`.
4. **One video driver.** Today night.py, producer VideoBridge and night `dispatch`/`pick_host` all exist. One driver plus a vidgen restart contract.
5. **One episode driver.** Today episode_run, lifecycle_tick, episode_pipeline, brain and sched all exist.
6. **Rule-version in job signatures**, with re-evaluation of existing takes on rule or config change. This covers the largest failure class.
7. **Move cross-host relays off the laptop.** These are relay_frames, shard_relay, lane_relay and asset_worker rendering through tunnels. The intent is in INFRA_LAPTOP_OFF_CRITICAL_PATH; the internal network trust decision is pending.
8. **Fail-closed and visible** instead of `arch-ok` fall-back-to-old-behaviour in scheduling code.
9. **Delete legacy artefacts** after sign-off: the 14 .bak files, data/producer/registry.json, untimed hold files, the config/netfix/partition.json legacy copy.
