## Video model and face-fix research for 3x3090 (as of 2026-10-29)

**Bottom line:** the cheapest and most likely fix stays inside MiniMax H3. Use an H3 face-refine pass, a people/realism LoRA, an H3 fine-tune aimed at faces, and better settings. All of that fits in about 20 GB of disk. LTX-2.5 is the strongest new open model with audio, but a full setup needs about 30–37 GB, which is more than the 28 GB free.

What I could not find:
- No Reddit or GitHub thread that measures eye stability. Every face claim below comes from a model author or a forum post.
- No 3090 timings for any of the newer models.
- No newer uncensored Qwen-Image derivative than NoctQ.
- Wan 2.5 through 3.0 and Qwen-Image 3.0 are API-only, with no open weights.

Licenses get one word each, as you asked. None of them affected the ranking.

### Why faces break in H3 now (Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3 discussion #30)
- **Small faces:** the smaller the head is on screen, the worse it gets.
- **Resolution:** one user found about 1.0 MP to be the "sweet spot". 576x1024 is about 0.59 MP. Other users found resolution made little difference, so this is disputed.
- **EasyCache:** reported to lower quality.
- **Latent upscalers:** they re-encode the video and lose detail.
- **Reported working settings:**
  - Turbo LoRA at 4 steps with strength 2.8, at 1.0 MP.
  - 8 steps with strength 1.8, or 12 steps with strength 1.0.
  - Or the DDIM sampler with the beta or beta57 scheduler at 8 steps.
- **Possible extra cause (my guess, untested):** the w4a8 DiT quantization. Comparing against an INT8 checkpoint would show whether it matters.
- Link: https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3/discussions/30

## Top 3 video models

**1. MiniMax H3 Singularity V2 INT8, with fal/MiniMax-H3-Realism-People-LoRA**
- **Repos:** `WarmBloodAban/Minimax-h3_Singularity` and `fal/MiniMax-H3-Realism-People-LoRA`.
- **Files:**
  - Singularity pruned INT8: 19.6 GB (recommended). The full build is 31.7 GB.
  - Realism LoRA: `h3-realism-people-t2v-i2v-r2v.safetensors`, size not listed. It is rank 32, so probably under 1 GB (unverified).
- **License:** Singularity says Apache-2.0, but it is derived from H3. The LoRA uses the H3-Community license.
- **What it is:** a merge of the H3 `ref`, `fl` and `b25-49` checkpoints, then fine-tuned and pruned.
  - Supports T2V, I2V, R2V and V2V, with audio as in H3.
  - Loads in the same slot as the stock H3 INT8 files, so it uses native ComfyUI H3 nodes.
  - INT8 runs well on Ampere.
- **VRAM and speed:** not stated. It should behave like H3 INT8. The `minimax_h3_ref2v_turbo_4step_v0.1` LoRA brings it to 4 steps.
- **Face claims:**
  - Singularity claims "distant face restoration", with less distortion and collapse in medium and long shots.
  - The LoRA claims coherent eyes and micro-expressions in close-ups, based on 19 same-seed before/after pairs. It uses the trigger word `r34l1sm` at strength 0.6–1.0.
  - Both are the authors' claims only. The LoRA is also reported to reduce a red-cheek drift in some cases (discussion #55).
- **NSFW:** not stated by either. It behaves like base H3, so treat it as unverified.
- **Why first:** it drops into the existing pipeline and keeps audio.
- Links:
  - https://huggingface.co/WarmBloodAban/Minimax-h3_Singularity
  - https://comfyui-wiki.com/en/news/2026-09-07-h3-singularity
  - https://huggingface.co/fal/MiniMax-H3-Realism-People-LoRA

**2. LTX-2.5 (Lightricks, 22B, audio and video), uncensored community merge**
- **Official files** (`Lightricks/LTX-2.5`, gated):
  - dev and distilled transformers in bf16: 42 GB each
  - comfy-int8-convrot transformers: 21.5 GB each
  - distilled nvfp4: 18.7 GB. This format needs Blackwell, so it does not suit a 3090.
  - Text encoder: Gemma-4 12B (bf16 or int8). The VAEs are about 1.8 GB.
- **License:** LTX-2.x Community.
- **Features:** native ComfyUI templates, I2V through frame-0 conditioning, synchronized audio, and a new decoder that Lightricks says gives "sharper faces".
- **Uncensored version** (`frtertaer/LTX-2.5-uncensored-v1.1-FP8`, Sept 2026, license "unknown"):
  - LTX-2.5 with the Eros10 NSFW LoRA, a DMD distill LoRA, an Img2Vid LoRA and IC-LoRAs merged in.
  - Transformer about 21.5 GB (fp8_scaled), uncensored Gemma-4 12B int8 text encoder 13.2 GB, VAEs about 1.8 GB. Total about 36.5 GB.
  - I2V uses 8–16 steps at CFG 1. It needs the ChrisColeTech ComfyUI-GGUF-Loader node.
  - On Ampere, fp8 is upcast, so it works but gets no speed gain.
- **Smaller GGUF option:** `chfm/LTX-2.5-Distilled-GGUF` or `Abiray/...`, Q4_K_M at 15.1–15.7 GB. With the 13.2 GB text encoder that is still about 30 GB, and these files are not uncensored.
- **Speed:** one RTX 3060 12 GB user reported a 15 s T2V clip at 960x544 in about 5 min. I found no 3090 figure. A 3090 is probably a few minutes per 6 s clip, but that is my extrapolation.
- **Faces:**
  - The `Alissonerdx/LTX-Best-Face-ID` LoRA plus the "LTX-2.5 Face Identity Reinforcer" node (ChrisColeTech) lock identity to a reference image. The evidence is the author's own A/B test. The LoRA was trained on 2.3, and its behaviour on 2.5 is untested.
  - Guides say morphing mostly comes from prompts that re-describe the image. Prompts should describe only motion, camera and audio.
- Links:
  - https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5
  - https://huggingface.co/frtertaer/LTX-2.5-uncensored-v1.1-FP8
  - https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5/discussions/25
  - https://comfy.icu/node/CCTechLTX25FaceIdentityReinforcer

## Top 2 face and eye fixes

**1. ComfyUI-H3-FaceRefine** (github.com/Carasibana/ComfyUI-H3-FaceRefine, v1.1.2)
- Works like FaceDetailer for H3 video: detects and tracks the face on every frame, crops it to a canvas (768 by default), regenerates it with the same H3 model at about 0.40 denoise, then composites it back with feathering and colour matching.
- Passes audio through and supports lipsync.
- Because it reuses H3, it handles whatever content H3 itself renders. There is no separate safety filter.
- **Extra disk:** only the `face_yolov8m.pt` detector (about 50 MB). InsightFace and SAM are optional.
- **Extra packages:** ultralytics, insightface, scenedetect. Install only one of onnxruntime or onnxruntime-gpu, or CUDA can silently drop out.
- **Cost:** VRAM grows with canvas² × frames.
- **Limits:** no eye-specific handling, and it can lock onto the wrong subject. It also helps only when the rest of the shot is fine.
- Related: t8star's "Face Refine" node in comfyui-minimax-h3-audio-T8, which is experimental.

**2. Settings, identity conditioning and LoRA (cheapest to try)**
- **H3 changes:**
  - Render at about 1.0 MP. For 9:16 that is roughly 768x1344 (my calculation).
  - Turbo LoRA at strength 1.8 with 8 steps.
  - Turn off EasyCache.
  - Try DDIM with the beta scheduler.
  - Add the Realism-People LoRA.
  - Use Ref2VA with high-resolution references in "max" mode. It is slower but holds identity better.
- **For LTX:** the Best-Face-ID LoRA plus the Reinforcer node described above.
- **Generic option:** ryukbk/ComfyUI-VideoFaceDetailer, a model-agnostic track, crop and resample node. Its author says it is unit-tested but not widely battle-tested.
- **Not relevant:** nikythebikky's Temporal Face Detailer is SDXL-only.
- Links:
  - https://github.com/ryukbk/ComfyUI-VideoFaceDetailer
  - https://www.instasd.com/post/minimax-h3-r2v-reference-to-video-comfyui

## Top 2 image models for first frames
I found nothing clearly newer and uncensored that beats NoctQ.

**1. Stay on NoctQ** (`Noctaluna/Noct-Q-Uncensored-Qwen-Image-2.1`, int8)
- License: qwen-research. The card is gated, so I could not see its files or version number.
- Reports say Qwen-Image 2.1 has no built-in filter to remove, so "uncensored" here means a fine-tune for content.
- Qwen-Image 3.0 (July 2026) is hosted-only, with no weights.
- No other uncensored Qwen-Image-2.1 fine-tune turned up. The other 2.1 repos are plain quantizations, such as Comfy-Org int8_convrot at 7.26 GB.

## What to try first (3x3090, about 28 GB free)
1. **Settings A/B, no download.** Run H3 at about 768x1344 (about 1 MP), Turbo LoRA strength 1.8 at 8 steps, EasyCache off. Put a 3090 on each variant in parallel.
2. **Add ComfyUI-H3-FaceRefine and the Realism-People LoRA.** About 0.1–1 GB. This is post-processing on the existing clips.
3. **Download Singularity V2 pruned INT8** (19.6 GB) and compare it with the w4a8 model using the same seed. This also tests whether w4a8 quantization is part of the problem.
   - Total for steps 1–3: about 21 GB, which fits in 28 GB.
4. **LTX-2.5 uncensored** only after freeing disk. It needs about 30 GB with Q4 GGUF or about 36.5 GB with fp8 plus the 13.2 GB text encoder.
   - Prefer the int8-convrot or GGUF files on Ampere.
   - Benchmark it on one 3090. All speed figures for it are unverified.

Wan 2.2 needs 25–35 GB and has no audio, so it ranks behind LTX for this pipeline.
