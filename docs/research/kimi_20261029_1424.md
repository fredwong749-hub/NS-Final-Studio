## Report: H3 model stack for the 60-episode vertical drama (research as of 2026-10-29)

**Access limits:** reddit.com is blocked to my fetcher, and civitai.com returns 403. User evidence therefore comes from HF discussions, civarchive pages, GitHub and blogs. Some NFAA-gated HF pages were only readable through the `/api/` endpoints. I did not try X.

### Main conclusions
1. **Finetunes probably won't fix anatomy, jewellery or skin.** Under a locked first frame, those come mostly from the first frame and the resolution, and the checkpoint matters little.
   - TenStrip (10Eros) admits his finetune "is not more NSFW than the H3 base" ([disc #7](https://huggingface.co/TenStrip/10Eros-Max/discussions/7)).
   - The PinkCherry author says H3 is "distilled… like turning brandy back into wine", that his checkpoint and the base give similar results, and that he has moved on to other models ([disc #26](https://huggingface.co/SexGod1979/PinkCherry-NSFW_MiniMax-H3/discussions/26)).
   - PinkCherry users on Oct 23 and Oct 29 report fine anatomical detail "missing or distorted even with references" (#29).
2. **FL2VA locks the first frame far better than Ref2VA.** The joeygambino multishot pack measured keyframe fidelity at **26.35 dB on fl2va versus 16.15 dB on ref2va**: "fl2va lands on a supplied frame, ref2va only nudges" ([link](https://huggingface.co/joeygambino/MiniMax-H3-Multishot-Workflow)). Ref2VA-based merges (PRISM, Singularity) and the beta6-style hybrids are therefore the wrong base for a locked frame. The beta6 thread "Terrible at Image to Video" (#63, Oct 12–14) reports lost likeness on beta6 while plain FL2VA kept it.
3. **No H3.1 and no new official open weights.**
   - H3.1 is not released ([apimaster, checked Sep 27](https://apimaster.ai/blog/minimax-h3-1-api)); I found nothing newer.
   - H3 Max is API-only (ComfyUI partner nodes, and fal's own post-trained version).

### Ranked recommendation (Ampere-safe, int8 ConvRot)
**1. Baseline. Keep the official FL2VA pruned int8_convrot you already have, plus these LoRAs:**
- **LightX2V FL2V 4-step v1.2, not v1.3.** One user (thread [#57](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Minimax-h3-Turbo/discussions/57)) reports v1.3 "severely degraded" when combined with sparse attention, Realism-People or Motion Repair LoRAs, and v1.2 fixed it. The developers acknowledged the issue. Settings: video shift 6, audio shift 3, euler, 4 steps. The alternative is 8-step v1.1 (no user feedback yet).
- **NEW: LiconStudio/Minimax-H3-VBVR, `H3-Licon-VBVR-I2V.safetensors` (~149 MB, Oct 23).** It is trained on the FL2VA bf16 base and targets object duplication, body-prop clipping and broken action order. That is directly relevant to holding jewellery counts. Evidence is only the author's six same-seed comparisons, and no strength is given, so start at 0.6–1.0 (unverified).
- **Your NSFW LoRAs at 0.2–0.6.** That is the range TenStrip uses for concept LoRAs.

**2. Challenger: 10Eros-Max beta7.** Released 2026-10-28, so it is a day old.
- **Files:**
  - `TenStrip/10Eros-Max` → `10Eros_Max_h3_8stepTURBO_beta7_int8HQ.safetensors` (22.9 GB). bf16 is 40.2 GB.
  - Smaller option: `LokkenJP/TenEros_Max_optimized_learned` → `10Eros_Max_h3_8stepTURBO_beta7_activation_learned_HQ.safetensors` (18.69 GB, W6 g16 + INT8). It needs ComfyUI ≥0.38 W6A8; sm_86 support is **unverified**.
- **What it is:** an fl2va model with the "Fast Omni timestep" refolded in. The author calls it the recommended version ("choose 5 or 7; avoid 6").
- **Settings:**
  - Shift **12/3** (confirmed in #71; the 10/3 setting was for beta6).
  - 8 steps, Euler/simple or er_sde/sgm_uniform; the turbo covers 6–12 steps.
  - VSA at about 90% sparsity is the author's recommendation. Whether VSA runs on sm_86 is **unverified** (likely a Triton fallback).
  - Lokken's two-pass workflow with a 3D latent upscale, because single-pass shows background flicker.
- **Evidence:**
  - One user calls it "best of 100+ models" (#76).
  - Another user reports constant background noise at 8 and 12 steps on bf16 (#76).
  - A third finds its audio weaker than beta5 and says it only works well with the upscaler workflow (#72).
  - No report yet on first-frame lock or explicit anatomy.

**3. PinkCherry v1.5 FL2VA.** `SexGod1979/PinkCherry-NSFW_MiniMax-H3/v1.5-fl2va/pinkcherry_v1.5_MiniMaxH3_fl2va_int8_pruned.safetensors` (21.2 GB, Oct 3).
- It is a pure FL2VA NSFW tune, so first-frame lock should hold.
- The author's recipe: FL2VA euler/simple template, LightX2V 8-step v1.0, about 0.8–2 MP.
- **v1.8-hybrid does exist:** `1.8-hybrid/PinkCherry_hybrid_v1.8_int8_minimax_h3_pruned.safetensors` (21.06 GB, Oct 8). It ships only a ref2va-turbo workflow, there are no user reports, and because it is a hybrid its FL behaviour is unknown.

**4. Use only for a Ref2VA identity pass, never as the FL base:**
- PRISM. Its HF repo now adds UPD1 `10Eros_Max_h3_8stepTURBO_beta7_SingularityV2_int8HQ` and UPD2 beta5×Singularity v1.3. A YouTube guide reports character duplication above about 0.5 MP, which your 9:16 frame at ~0.9 MP would hit.
- Singularity V2: int8 31.7 GB, Ref2VA.
- DaSiWa Hybrid v4: no v5 exists. int8 is 19.53 GB, last updated Oct 9, with zero user comments on quality.

### Identity across 60 episodes
- **Primary approach:** generate the first frame in Qwen with the heroine reference, then lock it in FL2VA. Other measured findings from the joeygambino pack:
  - Per-shot seeds held the face; one shared seed drifted face and voice.
  - Repeating character descriptions word-for-word across shots held a two-character 40 s scene with zero reference images.
  - Faces distort below about 1 MP, so render at 736×1280.
  - Wide shots break faces in general; HF [disc #30](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/MiniMax-H3/discussions/30) says to keep faces large.
- **First frame plus reference in one run:**
  - The stock nodes support `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` at frame_idx 0 together with Ref2VA references. Only the QuantFunc loader rejects this combination ([issue #19](https://github.com/QuantFunc/ComfyUI-QuantFunc/issues/19)).
  - Alternatively, entropicnoise's `MiniMaxH3CombinedImageAndReferenceToVideo` node (v1.6) pins first/middle/last frames and adds up to 10 unpinned `ref_images`.
  - This needs a model that reads references, i.e. a hybrid such as beta7, DaSiWa or PinkCherry 1.8. On Ref2VA bases the frame lock is weaker (point 2 above).
- **Post-pass:** H3-FaceRefine v1.1.2 tracks, crops, regenerates and stitches faces. It is not a face swap; identity comes from the references you give the H3 node. Defaults: 768 canvas, crop_factor 2.5, base denoise about 0.40.
  - akatz Character-Swap **V1.5** (`h3_character_swap_v1.5.safetensors`) replaces your pro4500 file. It runs on the Ref2VA int8 base with the 8-step turbo at 8 steps, er_sde/normal, CFG 1. Using it to restore identity on your own generated clips is **my inference; untested**.
- **H3 character LoRA:** feasible on a 3090 with **musubi-tuner** (`docs/minimax_h3.md`).
  - It supports FL2VA, Ref2VA and image mode.
  - The base must be the BF16 file (on-the-fly `--convrot_int8` is fine); pre-quantized int8 files can't be used. So a ~40 GB pruned BF16 download.
  - Memory: `--blocks_to_swap` up to 48 plus `--text_encoder_blocks_to_swap 50`. One SECourses report: 22.5 GB peak at 384 px / 124 frames on a 3090 (**unverified**).
  - Because H3 is CFG-distilled, use the `subject_ref` teacher-matching loss (learns identity from stills) or ostris's FL2VA training adapter v2. Example settings: rank 16, lr 3e-4, about 500 steps; the best checkpoint sits near the loss plateau.
  - ai-toolkit also works: 12 GB / 4070, about 31 face crops at 512, rank 16, 1000 steps, resemblance from about 800 steps. Only T2V was tested.
  - How well these LoRAs transfer to derivative checkpoints is unverified.
- **Qwen-Image 2.1 character LoRA:**
  - Use ai-toolkit; musubi did not support 2.1 as of 9/21.
  - Recipe: rank 16, lr 1e-4, 1024 px, 2000 steps, about 20–40 varied images, convrot8 quantization.
  - The one public 2.1 report (an anime character) got weak likeness.
  - Train on base 2.1 and test it on NoctQ (**unverified**). Fallback: native 2.1 multi-image reference editing (ComfyUI v0.37).

### First-frame image model
- **NoctQ is still the leader.**
  - The latest is **V4.0 Base** (civarchive updated Oct 20). There is **no V5**.
  - HF `Noctaluna/Noct-Q-Uncensored-Qwen-Image-2.1` has 591 likes and 318k downloads; everything else in the 2.1 merge tree is a re-upload.
  - Settings: 25 steps, euler/simple, CFG 3 with a negative prompt, 1024×1536.
- **Add `madebyollin/texture-fix-vae-for-qwen-image-2.1`** (Oct 5 update). It removes checkerboard artifacts and fp16 NaNs (rFID 3.38→2.07; PSNR slightly lower). Compatibility with finetunes is not stated.
- **Optional LoRA:** TheseAlpacas "NSFW LoRA" v2 for Qwen 2.1 (76 MB, updated Oct 29). Settings: 0.8–1.0, CFG 3–6, er_sde/beta. Users are mixed ("90% of cases" versus "no effect"). It may be the anatomy LoRA you already have.
- **Unverified alternatives:**
  - Small Screen Realism, a Qwen 2.1 merge (Oct 10, int8 6.7 GB), adult-capable per its author.
  - FLUX 3 Image Klein, 5.6B open weights (Oct 15); I found no NSFW finetunes.
- **Jewellery/piercing LoRAs:** I found **none** for Qwen 2.1 (search coverage was partial). Train your own detail LoRA, or build the jewellery into the first frame with inpainting.

### Getting a good 10 s clip
- H3 was designed for 15 s maximum (Lokken). 243 frames (17n+5) is a native length and fits one spoken line, while 124 frames does not.
- I found no measured comparison of 243 versus 141 frames. Keep camera moves and expression changes small; that is where drift is reported.
- **Dialogue speed:** keep Mandarin under about 3.5 characters/s, i.e. 30–34 characters per 10 s. One unscientific API test found about 83% accuracy, and syllables cut off at the end when lines are dense.
- **After the 2K pass:** lip-sync drifts 2–4 frames, so nudge the audio in post.
- **Prompt syntax:** the `<d>` tag syntax was **not found in any source**. Use punctuation, pinyin for polyphones, and a closing 。 on every line.
- **Speed tricks to avoid on people:** EasyCache, Spectrum and TeaCache cause visible distortion.

### A/B test on gpu3 (one ep01 shot)
Use the same Qwen first frame, the same prompt, 3 seeds, 736×1280, 243 frames.

| Variant | Model | LoRAs | Settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Official FL2VA int8 | LightX2V FL2V 4-step **v1.2** (1.0) + VBVR (0.8) + Unlocked V2.1 (0.4) | 4 steps, euler/simple, shift 6/3 |
| **B** | 10Eros beta7 int8HQ, FL node with first frame | none | 8 steps, er_sde/sgm_uniform, 12/3, Lokken two-pass (about 0.4 MP → 3D latent upscale → refine), Sol-Attn Ampere in place of VSA |
| **C** | Same as B | none | Combined node: first_frame pinned + heroine `<Picture 1>` as ref_image |
| **D** | PinkCherry v1.5 FL2VA int8 | LightX2V 8-step v1.0 | 8 steps, euler/simple |

- **Scoring:**
  - Frame-0 PSNR against the input image.
  - ArcFace similarity to the heroine reference at frames 0/60/120/180/240.
  - Jewellery count on 5 frames.
  - Background flicker, Mandarin lip-sync, wall time.
- **Then:** run FaceRefine on the winner as an E variant.
- **Extra disk:** about 44 GB (22.9 + 21.2 + 0.15).
